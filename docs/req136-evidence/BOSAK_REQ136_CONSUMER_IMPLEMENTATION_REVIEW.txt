## Findings and contract assessment

No blocking defect was found in the reviewed changes or executed consumer cases. Both Rename and whole-subtree Delete are delivered together. Existing creation APIs and formatting retain their contracts; the shared attribute pipeline change exposes existing scope/validation helpers internally without rewriting their behavior.

- Separate capabilities, operation-specific internally constructed targets, snapshot/reference membership and exclusive outcomes preserve authority. Equal-name Rename re-proves eligibility and returns NoChange without a candidate. Foreign/equal-byte identities refuse.
- Paired Rename changes the two actual name tokens, self-closing Rename changes one, and same-original edit replay is independently checked. Strict encoding coordinates and candidate offsets account for preceding length changes. Outside bytes, namespace declarations, attributes, descendants and lexical forms remain unchanged.
- Delete removes the full selected element and all descendants/attributes. Ordinary survivors, removed nodes and optional adjacent-whitespace contributors form exhaustive partitions; candidate nodes consist only of ordinary survivor images and actual inspected merged text. Selection is the mapped direct parent, and an emptied paired parent remains paired.
- Correspondence checks cover kinds, names, logical text, parent/child order, attribute metadata/order and translated extents. The merge proof preserves exact raw fragments while inspection supplies normalized candidate text, including entity/CRLF cases. Significant-text boundary merges explicitly refuse.
- Streaming XML preflight enforces the closed vocabulary and physical/depth/token/attribute bounds before candidate inspection and resource-capable validation. Shared restricted validation and denying candidate resolution retain the declared resource/static boundary. Iterative graph preflight precedes recursive proof. Source, caller-name and conservative complete-evidence budgets remain independent.
- Candidate exports are independent arrays; public collections and nested attribute-survivor dictionaries reject mutation. Concurrent proposals remain isolated. Cancellation is checked during bounded work and immediately before success/NoChange; unexpected faults are not broadly swallowed by the new pipeline.

The first profile remains no-namespace targets under creation-compatible literal ancestry, constant result attributes, the Legacy authoring host with explicit supported versions, strict supported encodings, and at most one whitespace-only boundary merge. Computed AVTs, targets beneath control instructions, preserved-space/CDATA and other excluded contexts remain unsupported. Complete lifecycle availability is the intersection of operation profiles, not every previously creatable element.
