# Separate immutable-source review — 5 November 2026

The pre-implementation [adoption record](SPEC-BASIS.md) remains unchanged. A later review of the dated [XSD 1.1 Datatypes Recommendation](https://www.w3.org/TR/2012/REC-xmlschema11-2-20120405/) confirms decimal in section 3.3.3 and integer in section 3.4.13. Integer derives from decimal, which supports checking integer before the broader decimal family.

The explicit authority protocol, strict primitive-kind match, operational guards and refusal policy are Core's selected host boundary. They are not W3C authentication rules or a new schema-validation implementation. No later specification draft or review date replaces the immutable adoption revision.
